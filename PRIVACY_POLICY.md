# Privacy Policy for AutoSpend

**Last Updated: January 17, 2026**

## Introduction

AutoSpend ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by AutoSpend.

## Our Commitment

**AutoSpend is a 100% offline application. All your financial data stays on your device and never leaves your phone.**

## Information Collection and Use

### Data We DO Collect (Locally on Your Device)

1. **SMS Messages**: AutoSpend reads SMS messages from banks and financial institutions to automatically detect transactions
   - Only SMS from known banks (matching bank sender ID patterns) are processed
   - Personal messages, OTPs, and other non-financial SMS are ignored
   - SMS data is processed locally and never transmitted

2. **Notifications**: With your permission, AutoSpend reads payment notifications from UPI apps (PhonePe, Google Pay, etc.)
   - Only payment-related notifications are processed
   - Other app notifications are ignored
   - Notification data is processed locally and never transmitted

3. **Transaction Data**: The app stores transaction information including:
   - Transaction amounts
   - Merchant names
   - Transaction dates and times
   - Categories
   - Payment sources

4. **Budget Data**: User-configured spending limits and alerts

5. **User Preferences**: App settings such as theme, currency, date format

### Data We DO NOT Collect

- ❌ We do not collect any personal identifying information
- ❌ We do not track your location
- ❌ We do not access your contacts
- ❌ We do not access your camera or photos
- ❌ We do not collect usage analytics or crash reports
- ❌ We do not use cookies or tracking technologies
- ❌ We do not require account creation or login

## Data Storage

- **All data is stored locally** in an encrypted SQLite database on your device
- **No cloud storage**: Your data never leaves your phone
- **No servers**: We do not operate any servers that collect or store your data
- **No backups to our servers**: Backups (when available) are stored locally on your device

## Data Sharing

**We do not share any data with third parties. Period.**

- No data transmission to external servers
- No data sharing with advertisers
- No data selling to data brokers
- No analytics providers
- No crash reporting services

## Permissions Required

### SMS Permission (Required)
- **Purpose**: Read bank transaction SMS to automatically capture transactions
- **What we read**: Only SMS from known banks matching transaction patterns
- **What we ignore**: Personal messages, OTPs, promotional SMS
- **Data handling**: Processed locally, never transmitted

### Notification Access (Optional)
- **Purpose**: Read UPI payment notifications for instant transaction capture
- **What we read**: Only payment notifications from UPI apps
- **What we ignore**: All other app notifications
- **Data handling**: Processed locally, never transmitted

### Storage Permission (Future Feature)
- **Purpose**: Export transaction data to your phone storage
- **Usage**: Only when you explicitly export data
- **Your control**: You choose where to save exported files

## Your Rights and Control

- **Full control**: You own all your data
- **Delete anytime**: Uninstalling the app removes all data from your device
- **Export data**: Export your transactions as CSV/JSON (feature in development)
- **No account needed**: No registration, no password, no account to delete
- **Revoke permissions**: You can revoke SMS/notification permissions anytime via Android settings

## Data Security

- **Local encryption**: Data is encrypted when biometric lock is enabled
- **No internet**: App works 100% offline
- **No data breaches possible**: Since we don't transmit data, there's no risk of server breaches
- **App lock**: Optional fingerprint/face unlock protects against unauthorized access

## Children's Privacy

AutoSpend is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date. Since the app is offline, you'll see policy updates when you update the app.

## Third-Party Services

AutoSpend does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or analytics tools. The app is completely self-contained.

## Data Retention

- Data is retained on your device until you:
  - Manually delete transactions
  - Reset the app
  - Uninstall the app
- We have no data retention policies since we don't collect data on servers

## International Data Transfers

Since all data stays on your device, there are no international data transfers.
